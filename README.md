## ⚙️ The Architecture: A Two-Step Agentic Workflow
If a trade appears on your screen, it has already passed strict risk-floor and profit-target gating. Your only job is to execute the plan.

*   **The Quantitative Discovery Scanner:** A high-speed engine that ruthlessly filters thousands of equities to find mathematically asymmetric risk-to-reward setups.
*   **Institutional Footprint Tracking:** The scanner looks beyond surface-grade price action by tracking institutional defense lines using Volume Profile (POC) and Anchored VWAP (AVWAP).
*   **Dynamic Risk Guardrails:** Setups are gated by strict structural parameters, including dynamic ATR-based risk management and a mandatory minimum 2:1 Reward-to-Risk ratio.
*   **The AI Auditor (The Devil's Advocate):** A qualitative AI analyst steps in to check the context of your quantitative setups. It audits for hidden traps like macroeconomic headwinds, upcoming earnings landmines, Altman Z-score distress signals, and insider trading activity before you risk capital.

## 📊 Proprietary Setup Classifications
Our engine reads the psychology and price action of the chart, categorizing opportunities into specific, graded momentum structures:

*   **Tight Consolidation (Coils):** Assets building energy against a ceiling with actively shrinking volatility.
*   **Post-Catalyst Bull Flags:** Tight trading ranges forming after a massive, high-volume surge.
*   **High-Volume Breakouts:** Momentum surges driven by daily volume at least 1.5x the normal average, signaling institutional accumulation

Every setup is systematically graded from *Speculative* to *Excellent (Actionable)* based on relative strength, risk-to-reward ratios, and price action triggers.
